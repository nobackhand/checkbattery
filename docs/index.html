Features

Small widget,
big clarity

BatteryPill does one thing and does it well. No bloat, no clutter, no system tray diving.

🔋
Always on top
Floats above all windows so your battery status is always one glance away.
⏱
Time remaining
Shows hours and minutes left, not just a percentage. Know exactly when you need to plug in.
📈
Detailed popup
Click the pill for charge rate, capacity, wear level, full runtime, and more.
💎
Crisp at any DPI
Renders perfectly on high-DPI and scaled displays. No blurry icons or clipped text.
🪶
Under 1 MB
A single lightweight executable. No installer, no dependencies, no background services.
👁
Drag anywhere
Place the pill wherever you want on screen. It remembers its position between sessions.

How it works

Up and running
in seconds

Download

Grab the latest release from GitHub. It's a single .exe file, no installer needed.

Run

Double-click to launch. The pill appears on your desktop and the tray icon sits quietly in the background.

Done

That's it. Drag the pill where you like. Click it for details. Right-click the tray icon to exit.
