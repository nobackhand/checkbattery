Features

  • Time, not percent Shows hours and minutes remaining so you know when to plug in.
  • Feels alive Fill levels glide, text crossfades, the pill glides with momentum when you fling it, and charging breathes.
  • Little moments Plug in and a bolt pops with your time-to-full. Hit 100% and the pill shimmers.
  • Native to Windows 11 Real DWM rounded corners and system shadows on every popup. Every animation has an off switch in Settings.
  • Live hover popup Big percentage, time remaining with ETA, and a 2-hour sparkline — updating while you watch.
  • Battery health A health-ring card showing wear, capacity, and how long this session has run on battery.
  • Customizable Dark/light/auto theme. 8 accent colors. 3 pill sizes. Fun status lines you can turn off.
  • Portable & light Single .exe, no installer, no dependencies. One battery query every 3 seconds; animations idle when nothing moves.

Install

  1. Download BatteryPill.exe from the button above
  2. Windows will likely show "Windows protected your PC" — that's SmartScreen reacting to an unsigned exe, not a virus verdict. BatteryPill is a free open source tool, and code-signing certificates cost hundreds of dollars a year. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  3. Double-click to run. The pill appears on your desktop.
  4. Drag it anywhere — fling it and it glides. Click it to switch time / percent. Right-click for settings.

Want it there after every reboot? Turn on "Start with Windows" in Settings.
Done with it? Exit from the tray icon, then delete the exe and the small .config.json next to it — that's the whole uninstall.
Heads-up: on PCs with Smart App Control enabled, Windows blocks unsigned apps outright (no "Run anyway"). We're honest about that too — code signing is on the roadmap.
