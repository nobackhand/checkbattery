Features

Small widget,
big clarity

BatteryPill does one thing and does it well. No bloat, no clutter, no system tray diving.

Always on top
Floats above all windows so your battery status is always one glance away.
Time remaining
Shows hours and minutes left, not just a percentage. Know exactly when you need to plug in.
Detailed popup
Hover over the pill for charge rate, capacity, wear level, battery history, and more.
Crisp at any DPI
Renders perfectly on high-DPI and scaled displays. No blurry icons or clipped text.
Battery sparkline
See your battery trend over the last 2 hours. Charging periods highlighted in green, with a 50% guide line.
Fully customizable
Dark, light, or auto theme. 8 accent colors. Compact, normal, or expanded pill. Snap-to-edge dragging. All persisted across sessions.

How it works

Up and running
in seconds

Download

Grab the latest version from this page. It's a single .exe file, no installer needed.

Run

Double-click to launch. The pill appears on your desktop and the tray icon sits quietly in the background.

Done

That's it. Drag the pill where you like. Click it for details. Right-click the tray icon to exit.
