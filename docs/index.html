Features

  • Always on top Floats above all windows. Auto-hides in fullscreen apps.
  • Time, not percent Shows hours and minutes remaining so you know when to plug in.
  • Hover popup Big percentage up top, plus charge rate, capacity, wear, ETA, and a 2-hour sparkline.
  • DPI aware Renders crisp on any display scaling. No blurry text.
  • Customizable Dark/light/auto theme. 8 accent colors. 3 pill sizes.
  • Portable Single .exe, no installer, no dependencies. Config saved locally.

Install

  1. Download BatteryPill-1.1.2.exe from the button above
  2. Double-click to run. The pill appears on your desktop.
  3. Drag it anywhere. Click it to switch time / percent. Right-click for settings.

Want it there after every reboot? Turn on "Start with Windows" in Settings.
Done with it? Exit from the tray icon, then delete the exe and the small .config.json next to it — that's the whole uninstall.
